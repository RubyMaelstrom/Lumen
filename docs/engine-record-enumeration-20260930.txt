The implementation validates an ordinary, non-callable, non-exotic receiver

reuse; the cache holds no objects, layouts, descriptors or JS values. Queue
metadata remains bounded by the existing ByteLru compaction policy. The
managed-memory census visits both string owners and cache metadata, marking
opaque HashMap storage as a lower bound. No tier admission or object/value
representation changes; all ordinary tiers share the builtin implementation.
